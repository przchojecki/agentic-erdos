# EP-693

## Problem Statement
Let $k\geq 2$ and $n$ be sufficiently large depending on $k$. Let $A=\{a_1<a_2<\cdots \}$ be the set of those integers in $[n,n^k]$ which have a divisor in $(n,2n)$. Estimate\[\max_{i} a_{i+1}-a_i.\]Is this $\leq (\log n)^{O(1)}$?

## Local Computation
- Implemented direct finite scans for \(k=2,3\) across moderate \(n\), explicitly constructing \(A\) and measuring maximum consecutive gap.

- For tested ranges, the maximal gaps are modest (small constants / low tens), and normalized by \(\log n\) remain controlled in finite data.
- This is compatible with polylogarithmic-scale optimism on these sizes, but far from asymptotic proof.

## Local Proof Attempts
The finite attack directly constructs the set for \(k=2,3\) over moderate \(n\)-ranges and measures its largest consecutive gap. Those scans show only modest finite gaps and are compatible with polylogarithmic growth, but they do not provide a mechanism that would control the gap uniformly in \(n\) or across all \(k\). The problem remains open.
