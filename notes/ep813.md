# EP-813

## Problem Statement
Let $h(n)$ be minimal such that every graph on $n$ vertices where every set of $7$ vertices contains a triangle (a copy of $K_3$) must contain a clique on at least $h(n)$ vertices. Estimate $h(n)$ - in particular, do there exist constants $c_1,c_2>0$ such that\[n^{1/3+c_1}\ll h(n) \ll n^{1/2-c_2}?\]

## Site Remarks
A problem of Erd\H{o}s and Hajnal, who could prove that\[n^{1/3}\ll h(n) \ll n^{1/2}.\]Buci\'{c} and Sudakov \cite{BuSu23} have proved\[h(n) \gg n^{5/12-o(1)}.\]

## Local Computation
- Implemented deeper one-by-one finite search over random dense graphs.
- For each tested $n$, optimized candidate quality by minimizing sampled violations of the local 7-set triangle condition.
- On best candidates, computed clique number exactly via branch-and-bound over bitmasks.
- No full finite witness satisfying the local condition was found in these runs.

- Best sampled candidates at $n=26,30,34,38$ still had nonzero sampled violations.
- Exact clique numbers on best candidates were 7, 7, 7, 8 respectively.
- The finite search is informative about scale but does not yet yield structural proof input.

## Local Proof Attempts
The finite search tries to build dense graphs that nearly satisfy the local 7-vertex triangle condition and then measures their clique number exactly. That gives some scale information, but the search never reached a true witness, so it does not yet connect to the structural extremal question for \(h(n)\).
