# EP-700

## Problem Statement
Let\[f(n)=\min_{1<k\leq n/2}\textrm{gcd}\left(n,\binom{n}{k}\right).\]{UL}
{LI}Characterise those composite $n$ such that $f(n)=n/P(n)$, where $P(n)$ is the largest prime dividing $n$.{/LI}
{LI}Are there infinitely many composite $n$ such that $f(n)>n^{1/2}$?{/LI}
{LI} Is it true that, for every composite $n$,\[f(n) \ll_A \frac{n}{(\log n)^A}\]for every $A>0$?{/LI}
{/UL}

## Site Remarks
A problem of Erd\H{o}s and Szekeres. It is easy to see that $f(n)\leq n/P(n)$ for composite $n$, since if $j=p^k$ where $p^k\mid n$ and $p^{k+1}\nmid n$ then $\textrm{gcd}\left(n,\binom{n}{j}\right)=n/p^k$. This implies\[f(n) \leq (1+o(1))\frac{n}{\log n}.\]It is known that $f(n)=n/P(n)$ when $n$ is the product of two primes. Another example is $n=30$.

For the second problem, it is easy to see that for any $n$ we have $f(n)\geq p(n)$, where $p(n)$ is the smallest prime dividing $n$, and hence there are infinitely many $n$ (those $=p^2)$ such that $f(n)\geq n^{1/2}$.

## Local Computation
- Exact standalone scan for all composite \(n\le N\):
 - factored each \(n\),
 - computed \(p\)-adic valuations of \(\binom{n}{k}\) for each \(k\),
 - evaluated the exact minimum gcd value \(f(n)\).
- Tracked frequencies of equality \(f(n)=n/P(n)\) and threshold exceedance \(f(n)>\sqrt n\).

- Deep run to \(N=12000\) (10561 composite integers scanned).
- At \(n\le 12000\):
 - fraction with \(f(n)=n/P(n)\) about \(0.5799\),
 - fraction with \(f(n)>\sqrt n\) about \(0.0471\).
- Maximum observed ratio \(f(n)/(n/P(n))\) remained 1 in this range (consistent with the upper bound).

## Local Proof Attempts
The finite route was to compute \(f(n)\) exactly for every composite \(n\) in a long initial range by evaluating the \(p\)-adic content of \(\binom{n}{k}\) across all admissible \(k\). This cleanly measures how often the upper bound \(f(n)\le n/P(n)\) is sharp and how often \(f(n)>\sqrt n\) occurs, but it does not yet reveal the structural criterion for equality or the full size behavior of \(f(n)\).
