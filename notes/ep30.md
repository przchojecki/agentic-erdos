# EP-30

## Problem Statement
Let $h(N)$ be the maximum size of a Sidon set in $\{1,\ldots,N\}$. Is it true that, for every $\epsilon>0$,\[h(N) = N^{1/2}+O_\epsilon(N^\epsilon)?\]

## Site Remarks
A problem of Erd\H{o}s and Tur\'{a}n. It may even be true that $h(N)=N^{1/2}+O(1)$, but Erd\H{o}s remarks this is perhaps too optimistic. Erd\H{o}s and Tur\'{a}n \cite{ErTu41} proved an upper bound of $N^{1/2}+O(N^{1/4})$, with an alternative proof by Lindstr\"{o}m \cite{Li69}. Both proofs in fact give\[h(N) \leq N^{1/2}+N^{1/4}+1.\]Balogh, F\"{u}redi, and Roy \cite{BFR21} improved the bound in the error term to $0.998N^{1/4}$. This was further optimised by O'Bryant \cite{OB22}. The current record is\[h(N)\leq N^{1/2}+0.98183N^{1/4}+O(1),\]due to Carter, Hunter, and O'Bryant \cite{CHO25}.

Singer \cite{Si38} was the first to show that $h(N)\geq (1-o(1))N^{1/2}$ for all $N$. For a detailed survey of the literature we refer to \cite{OB04}.

See also [241] and [840].

This problem is Problem 31 on Green's open problems list (https://people.maths.ox.ac.uk/greenbj/papers/open-problems.pdf).

This is discussed in problem C9 of Guy's collection \cite{Gu04}.

## Local Computation
Finite compute signal:
 - Structured $N=q^2+q+1$ profile (prime $q$) gives lower bound size $q+1=\sqrt{N}+O(1)$, numerically near $\sqrt{N}+0.5$ (ratio to $\sqrt{N}$ near $1.001$ at $q=509$).

 - Computation supports strong near-$\sqrt{N}$ lower-scale behavior; the open part is tightening the upper error to $N^\epsilon$ (or $O(1)$).

## Local Proof Attempts
Target:
Sharpen the asymptotic around \(\sqrt N\)-scale in EP-30 to the conjectured
error regime (e.g. \(N^\varepsilon\) or \(O(1)\), depending on the precise form).
Step A: Extremal Lower Construction Rigidity
Show known projective-plane-type lower constructions are essentially extremal at
first order for infinitely many \(N\).
Step B: Upper Bound Compression
Improve current upper error term (around \(N^{1/4}\)-type in this note context)
by a density-increment or incidence argument.
Step C: Match Error Exponents
Iterate Step B until upper error matches lower construction scale.
Concrete Blocking Lemma (Most Critical):
Need a refined incidence bound that eliminates medium-scale concentration
patterns responsible for the \(N^{1/4}\)-barrier in current upper estimates.

Let \(Q(N)\) denote the extremal quantity in EP-30.
Proposition B1 (Asymptotic conclusion from matched bounds):
Suppose there exist functions \(L(N),U(N)\) with
\[
L(N)\le Q(N)\le U(N),
\]
and
\[
L(N)=\sqrt N+\ell(N),\qquad U(N)=\sqrt N+u(N),
\]
where \(\ell(N),u(N)=o(\sqrt N)\). Then
\[
Q(N)=\sqrt N+o(\sqrt N).
\]
\[
\textit{Proof.}
\]
Subtract \(\sqrt N\):
\[
\ell(N)\le Q(N)-\sqrt N\le u(N).
\]
Since both endpoints are \(o(\sqrt N)\), squeeze gives
\[
Q(N)-\sqrt N=o(\sqrt N).
\]
\(\square\)
Proposition B2 (Constant-error regime implies sharp first-order asymptotic):
If one improves to
\[
\sqrt N - C_1 \le Q(N)\le \sqrt N + C_2
\]
for absolute constants \(C_1,C_2\), then
\[
Q(N)=\sqrt N+O(1).
\]
\[
\textit{Proof.}
\]
Immediate from two-sided inequality.
\(\square\)
These are rigorous implication steps; the genuinely open part is proving the
needed upper/lower bounds themselves at the conjectured error scale.
