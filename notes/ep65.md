# EP-65

## Problem Statement
Let $G$ be a graph with $n$ vertices and $kn$ edges, and $a_1<a_2<\cdots $ be the lengths of cycles in $G$. Is it true that\[\sum\frac{1}{a_i}\gg \log k?\]Is the sum $\sum\frac{1}{a_i}$ minimised when $G$ is a complete bipartite graph?

## Site Remarks
A problem of Erd\H{o}s and Hajnal.

Gy\'{a}rf\'{a}s, Koml\'{o}s, and Szemer\'{e}di \cite{GKS84} have proved that this sum is $\gg \log k$, so that only the second question remains. Liu and Montgomery \cite{LiMo20} have proved the asymptotically sharp lower bound of $\geq (\tfrac{1}{2}-o(1))\log k$.

Montgomery has written a survey (https://ems.press/content/serial-article-files/52107) including this problem, in which he mentions forthcoming work of himself, Milojevi\'{c}, Pokrovskiy, and Sudakov which proves that, if $k$ is sufficiently large, then $\sum\frac{1}{a_i}$ is maximised when $G$ is a complete bipartite graph.

This problem is #65 in Extremal Graph Theory (https://mathweb.ucsd.edu/~erdosproblems/erdos/newproblems/HarmonicSumOfCycleLengths.html) in the graphs problem collection.

See also [57].

## Local Proof Attempts
Target:
Close the remaining extremal minimizer part after the known
\[
\sum \frac1{a_i}\gg \log k
\]
lower bounds.
Step A: Near-extremal characterization
Classify families achieving
\[
\sum_{i=1}^k \frac1{a_i}\le \left(\frac12+\varepsilon\right)\log k
\]
as perturbations of a canonical minimizer template.
Step B: Stability of reciprocal sum
Prove deviations from template incur explicit additive penalty in reciprocal sum.
Step C: Uniqueness / exact asymptotics
Deduce asymptotically sharp minimizer structure and constant.
Concrete Blocking Lemma (Most Critical):
Need a reciprocal-stability inequality: if local spacing/cover constraints differ
from template on proportion \(\rho\), then reciprocal sum increases by
\(\ge c\rho\log k\).

Proposition R1 (Stability implies asymptotic optimality):
If every near-minimizer is \(o(k)\)-edit-close to template \(T_k\), and
\[
\sum_{a\in T_k}\frac1a = \left(\frac12+o(1)\right)\log k,
\]
then the extremal minimum has the same asymptotic.
\[
\textit{Proof.}
\]
By closeness, reciprocal sums differ by \(o(\log k)\) under the stability
penalty bound; combine with template value.
\(\square\)
Proposition R2 (Uniqueness up to \(o(k)\)-edits):
Under strict stability penalty, any two minimizers differ by \(o(k)\) elements.
\[
\textit{Proof.}
\]
If not, penalty would force one to have strictly larger objective than minimum.
\(\square\)
