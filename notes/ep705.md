# EP-705

## Problem Statement
Let $G$ be a finite unit distance graph in $\mathbb{R}^2$ (i.e. the vertices are a finite collection of points in $\mathbb{R}^2$ and there is an edge between two points if and only if the distance between them is $1$).

Is there some $k$ such that if $G$ has girth $\geq k$ (i.e. $G$ contains no cycles of length $<k$) then $\chi(G)\leq 3$?

## Site Remarks
The maximal value of $\chi(G)$ (without a girth condition) is the Hadwiger-Nelson problem (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hadwiger%E2%80%93Nelson_problem). There are unit distance graphs (e.g. the Moser spindle) with $\chi(G)=4$ of girth $3$. de Grey \cite{dG18} has constructed a unit distance graph $G$ with $\chi(G)=5$. (I do not know what the largest girth achieved is by these recent constructions.)

Wormald \cite{Wo79} has constructed a unit distance graph with $\chi(G)=4$ and girth $5$, with $6448$ vertices. O'Donnell \cite{OD94} has constructed a unit distance graph with $\chi(G)=4$ and girth $4$, with $56$ vertices. Chilakamarri \cite{Ch95} has constructed an infinite family of unit distance graphs with $\chi(G)=4$ and girth $4$, the smallest of which has $47$ vertices.

The general case was solved by O'Donnell \cite{OD99}, who constructed finite unit distance graphs with chromatic number $4$ and arbitrarily large girth.

See also [508], [704], and [706].

## Local Computation
- Verified exact properties of the classical 7-vertex Moser spindle witness:
 - exact chromatic number via backtracking coloring,
 - exact girth via BFS cycle search.
- This does not itself give large girth, but confirms the fundamental 4-chromatic unit-distance obstruction.

- For the Moser spindle: \(|V|=7\), \(|E|=11\), \(\chi=4\), girth \(=3\).
- Combined with modern large-girth constructions from the literature, this closes the original question negatively.

## Local Proof Attempts
This note is closed negatively. The local verification checks the Moser spindle exactly as a concrete 4-chromatic unit-distance obstruction, and the literature now supplies arbitrarily large-girth unit-distance graphs with chromatic number at least 4. So no threshold \(k\) can force \(\chi(G)\le 3\) from girth\((G)\ge k\).
