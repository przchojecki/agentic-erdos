# EP-1013

## Problem Statement
Let $h_3(k)$ be the minimal $n$ such that there exists a triangle-free graph on $n$ vertices with chromatic number $k$. Find an asymptotic for $h_3(k)$, and also prove\[\lim_{k\to \infty}\frac{h_3(k+1)}{h_3(k)}=1.\]

## Site Remarks
The function $h_3(k)$ is dual to the function $f(n)$ considered in [1104], in that $h_3(k)= n$ if and only if $n$ is minimal such that $f(n)=k$.

Graver and Yackel \cite{GrYa68} proved\[h_3(k)\gg \frac{\log k}{\log\log k}k^2.\]The bounds for $f(n)$ from [1104] imply\[\left(\frac{1}{2}-o(1)\right)k^2\log k\leq h_3(k) \leq (1+o(1))k^2\log k.\]See also [920] for a generalisation to $K_r$-free graphs.

## Site References

- 13:27 on 21 Jan 2026 - Wouter CvB (reply depth 2):
  For the record: as in problem 1104, the application of Johansson's theorem is more subtle. It says that $\chi(G)\ll \frac{\Delta}{ \log \Delta}$ where $\Delta$ is the maximum degree of a triangle-free graph $G$. This implies $\chi(G)\ll \sqrt {\frac{n}{\log n}}$, hence $h_3(k) \gg k^2 \log k$. The proof in a nutshell: If $\Delta \leq \sqrt{n \log n}$ then it's immediate. Otherwise select a vertex $v$ with maximum degree and apply induction to $G-N[v]$.

## Local Proof Attempts
Target:
Determine asymptotics of \(h_3(k)\), the minimum order of triangle-free \(k\)-chromatic graphs.
A. Concrete Blocking Lemma (Most Critical):
Prove a sharp stability lower bound:
\[
h_3(k)\ge c\,k^2\log k + O(k^2)
\]
with matching construction constant \(c\), plus structure of near-extremal witnesses.
B. Reduction Lemmas (with proofs):
Lemma B1 (monotonicity):
\[
h_3(k+1)\ge h_3(k)+1.
\]
Proof.
A \((k+1)\)-chromatic graph cannot have the same order as a minimal \(k\)-chromatic witness unless that witness were already \((k+1)\)-chromatic; minimality and strict chromatic increase force at least one extra vertex. \(\square\)
Lemma B2 (Mycielski upper recursion):
\[
h_3(k+1)\le 2h_3(k)+1.
\]
Proof.
Apply Mycielski construction to a minimal triangle-free \(k\)-chromatic graph. It preserves triangle-freeness and increases chromatic number by one with the stated vertex count. \(\square\)
Lemma B3 (A implies ratio smoothness):
If \(h_3(k)=c k^2\log k(1+o(1))\), then
\[
\frac{h_3(k+1)}{h_3(k)}\to 1.
\]
Proof.
Evaluate quotient of asymptotic expressions at \(k+1\) and \(k\); the relative change of \(k^2\log k\) is \(1+o(1)\). \(\square\)
C. Main unresolved point:
The missing ingredient is a full extremal characterization of near-minimal triangle-free \(k\)-chromatic graphs to lock constants and second-order terms.
