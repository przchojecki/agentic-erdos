# EP-544

## Problem Statement
Show that\[R(3,k+1)-R(3,k)\to\infty\]as $k\to \infty$. Similarly, prove or disprove that\[R(3,k+1)-R(3,k)=o(k).\]

## Site Remarks
A problem of Erd\H{o}s and S\'{o}s. It is known (see [165]) that\[R(3,k) \asymp \frac{k^2}{\log k}.\]The OpenAI bound of [1014] implies that\[R(3,k+1)-R(3,k)\ll k^{-c}R(3,k)\]for some constant $c>0$.

This problem is #8 in Ramsey Theory (https://mathweb.ucsd.edu/~erdosproblems/erdos/newproblems/R3nBehavior.html) in the graphs problem collection.

See also [165] and [1014].
