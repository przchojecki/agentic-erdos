# EP-14

## Problem Statement
Let $A\subseteq \mathbb{N}$. Let $B\subseteq \mathbb{N}$ be the set of integers which are representable in exactly one way as the sum of two elements from $A$.

Is it true that for all $\epsilon>0$ and large $N$\[\lvert \{1,\ldots,N\}\backslash B\rvert \gg_\epsilon N^{1/2-\epsilon}?\]Is it possible that\[\lvert \{1,\ldots,N\}\backslash B\rvert =o(N^{1/2})?\]

## Site Remarks
Apparently originally considered by Erd\H{o}s and Nathanson, although later Erd\H{o}s attributes this to Erd\H{o}s, S\'{a}rk\"{o}zy, and Szemer\'{e}di (but gives no reference), and claims a construction of an $A$ such that for all $\epsilon>0$ and all large $N$\[\lvert \{1,\ldots,N\}\backslash B\rvert \ll_\epsilon N^{1/2+\epsilon},\]and yet there for all $\epsilon>0$ there exist infinitely many $N$ where\[\lvert \{1,\ldots,N\}\backslash B\rvert \gg_\epsilon N^{1/3-\epsilon}.\]Erd\"{o}s and Freud investigated the finite analogue in \cite{ErFr91}, proving that there exists $A\subseteq \{1,\ldots,N\}$ such that the number of integers not representable in exactly one way as the sum of two elements from $A$ is $<2^{3/2}N^{1/2}$, and suggest the constant $2^{3/2}$ is perhaps best possible.

## Site References

## Local Computation
Route:
Performed heuristic local search for sets A subseteq [1,N] with |A|=floor(sqrt(N)) minimizing counts of integers not represented in exactly one way as a sum of two elements of A.

## Local Proof Attempts
Evidence from this batch:
- gives best bad-counts 68, 97, 146, 215 for N=120,180,260,360.
- These are consistent with a sqrt(N)-scale error profile in this range.

Hard point:
Heuristic finite constructions do not settle sharp asymptotic exponents/constants in the infinite problem.

Target:
Determine sharp asymptotics for the minimum number of integers lacking unique
representation as \(a+a'\) with \(a,a'\in A\), \(|A|=\lfloor\sqrt N\rfloor\).
Step A: Extremal Configuration Structure
Classify near-optimal \(A\subset[1,N]\) with minimal non-unique/absent counts as
perturbations of structured templates (quasi-Sidon-with-density constraints).
Step B: Lower Bound via Additive Energy
Prove any \(A\) of this size must incur at least
\[
c\sqrt N - o(\sqrt N)
\]
bad integers through energy/representation multiplicity constraints.
Step C: Matching Upper Construction
Construct families with
\[
c\sqrt N + o(\sqrt N)
\]
bad integers, matching Step B constant.
Concrete Blocking Lemma (Most Critical):
Need a mixed Sidon-density inequality linking additive energy of \(A\) to count
of uniquely represented sums in the interval model, with optimal \(\sqrt N\)-scale constants.
