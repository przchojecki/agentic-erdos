# EP-986

## Problem Statement
For any fixed $k\geq 3$,\[R(k,n) \gg \frac{n^{k-1}}{(\log n)^c}\]for some constant $c=c(k)>0$.

## Site Remarks
Spencer \cite{Sp77} proved this for $k=3$ and Mattheus and Verstraete \cite{MaVe23} proved this for $k=4$.

The best general bounds available are\[\frac{n^{\frac{k+1}{2}}}{(\log n)^{\frac{1}{k-2}-\frac{k+1}{2}}}\ll_k R(k,n) \ll_k \frac{n^{k-1}}{(\log n)^{k-2}}.\]The lower bound was proved by Bohman and Keevash \cite{BoKe10}. The upper bound was proved by Ajtai, Koml\'{o}s, and Szemer\'{e}di \cite{AKS80}. Li, Rousseau, and Zang \cite{LRZ01} have shown that $\ll_k$ in the upper bound can be improved to $\leq (1+o(1))$.

The special case $k=3$ is the topic of [165] and $k=4$ is the topic of [166].

This problem is #6 in Ramsey Theory (https://mathweb.ucsd.edu/~erdosproblems/erdos/newproblems/Rkn.html) in the graphs problem collection.

See also [920].

## Local Proof Attempts
For \(k=3\), the finite witnesses behave as expected for the \(R(3,t)\) lower-bound problem: triangle-free graphs with relatively small independence number continue to appear at larger \(n\). The general fixed-\(k\) lower-bound problem remains open, and these computations only provide explicit finite witnesses rather than an asymptotic construction.
