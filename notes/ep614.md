# EP-614

## Problem Statement
Let $f(n,k)$ be minimal such that there is a graph with $n$ vertices and $f(n,k)$ edges where every set of $k+2$ vertices induces a subgraph with maximum degree at least $k$. Determine $f(n,k)$.

## Forum Comments
- 13:30 on 07 Jan 2026 - Quanyu Tang:
  Ma and I wrote a short note (https://github.com/QuanyuTang/erdos-614-turan-reduction/blob/main/erdos614_note.pdf) on this problem, showing that it can be reformulated as a finite-family Turán problem.
  More precisely, for each fixed $k$ there exists a finite family of graphs $\mathcal F_k^{\min}$ (depending only on $k$) such that\[
  f(n,k)=\binom{n}{2}-\mathrm{ex}\bigl(n,\mathcal F_k^{\min}\bigr).
  \]In particular, for $k=1,2,3$ we obtain\[
  f(n,1)=\binom{n}{2}-\mathrm{ex}(n,K_3)=\binom{n}{2}-\left\lfloor\frac{n^2}{4}\right\rfloor,\]\[
  f(n,2)=\binom{n}{2}-\mathrm{ex}(n,C_4),\]and\[
  f(n,3)=\binom{n}{2}-\mathrm{ex}\bigl(n,\{C_5,\,K_{2,3},\,K_1\vee 2K_2\}\bigr),\]where $K_1\vee 2K_2$ denotes the join of a single vertex with two disjoint edges.
  We do not know whether this Turán-type reformulation is already what Erdős had in mind as an ''answer'' for $f(n,k)$.
