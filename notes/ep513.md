# EP-513

## Problem Statement
Let $f=\sum_{n=0}^\infty a_nz^n$ be a transcendental entire function. What is the greatest possible value of\[\liminf_{r\to \infty} \frac{\max_n\lvert a_nr^n\rvert}{\max_{\lvert z\rvert=r}\lvert f(z)\rvert}?\]

## Site Remarks
Let $B$ be the supremum of all such values.

It is trivial that $B\in [1/2,1]$. K\"{o}v\'{a}ri (unpublished) observed that it must be $>1/2$. Gray and Shah \cite{GrSh63} give an argument of Clunie which shows $B\leq 2/\pi$. Clunie and Hayman \cite{ClHa64} improved both bounds to\[4/7< B \leq 2/\pi-c\]for some absolute constant $c>0$. He and Tang \cite{HeTa26} have improved the lower bound to\[B> 0.5850724\](note that $4/7\approx 0.57143$). This was further improved slightly to $0.5850788$ by GPT (as prompted by Sothanaphan). Note that $2/\pi \approx 0.63662$.

See also [227].
