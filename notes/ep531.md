# EP-531

## Problem Statement
Let $F(k)$ be the minimal $N$ such that if we two-colour $\{1,\ldots,N\}$ there is a set $A$ of size $k$ such that all subset sums $\sum_{a\in S}a$ (for $\emptyset\neq S\subseteq A$) are monochromatic. Estimate $F(k)$.

## Site Remarks
The existence of $F(k)$ was established by Sanders and Folkman, and it also follows from Rado's theorem. It is commonly known as Folkman's theorem (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Folkman%27s_theorem).

Erd\H{o}s and Spencer \cite{ErSp89} proved that\[F(k) \geq 2^{ck^2/\log k}\]for some constant $c>0$. Balogh, Eberhrad, Narayanan, Treglown, and Wagner \cite{BENTW17} have improved this to\[F(k) \geq 2^{2^{k-1}/k}.\]
