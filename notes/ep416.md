# EP-416

## Problem Statement
Let $V(x)$ count the number of $n\leq x$ such that $\phi(m)=n$ is solvable. Does $V(2x)/V(x)\to 2$? Is there an asymptotic formula for $V(x)$?

## Site Remarks
Pillai \cite{Pi29} proved $V(x)=o(x)$. Erd\H{o}s \cite{Er35b} proved $V(x)=x(\log x)^{-1+o(1)}$.

The behaviour of $V(x)$ is now almost completely understood. Maier and Pomerance \cite{MaPo88} proved\[V(x)=\frac{x}{\log x}e^{(C+o(1))(\log\log\log x)^2},\]for some explicit constant $C>0$. Ford \cite{Fo98} improved this to\[V(x)\asymp\frac{x}{\log x}e^{C_1(\log\log\log x-\log\log\log\log x)^2+C_2\log\log\log x-C_3\log\log\log\log x}\]for some explicit constants $C_1,C_2,C_3>0$. Unfortunately this falls just short of an asymptotic formula for $V(x)$ and determining whether $V(2x)/V(x)\to 2$.

In \cite{Er79e} Erd\H{o}s asks further to estimate the number of $n\leq x$ such that the smallest solution to $\phi(m)=n$ satisfies $kx<m\leq (k+1)x$.

See also [417] and [821].

This is discussed in problem B36 of Guy's collection \cite{Gu04}.

## Local Proof Attempts
Target:
Prove precise asymptotics for
\[
V(x)=\#\{n\le x:\exists m,\ \phi(m)=n\},
\]
including whether \(V(2x)/V(x)\to2\).
A. Concrete Blocking Lemma (Most Critical):
Establish a uniform short-interval totient-value theorem:
\[
V(x+h)-V(x)=\kappa\,\frac{h}{(\log x)^{\alpha}}(1+o(1))
\]
for \(h\in[x^\theta,x]\) with fixed \(\theta<1\), enough to integrate to global ratio.
B. Reduction Lemmas (with proofs):
Lemma B1 (ratio from asymptotic):
If \(V(x)\sim F(x)\) with regularly varying \(F\) of index \(1\), then \(V(2x)/V(x)\to2\).
Proof.
Regular variation gives \(F(2x)/F(x)\to2\), and asymptotic equivalence transfers ratio to \(V\). \(\square\)
Lemma B2 (summation from local increments):
Asymptotic for increments in A implies asymptotic for \(V(x)\) by summation/integration over dyadic blocks.
Proof.
Sum local increment formula over partition of \([1,x]\); error terms are controlled by uniformity in \(h\). \(\square\)
Lemma B3 (A implies EP-416 ratio claim):
Combine B2 then B1 to conclude \(V(2x)/V(x)\to2\).
Proof.
Direct composition. \(\square\)
C. Main unresolved point:
The key obstacle is proving sufficiently uniform local distribution of totient values in short intervals.
