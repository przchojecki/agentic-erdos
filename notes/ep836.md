# EP-836

## Problem Statement
Let $r\geq 2$ and $G$ be a $r$-uniform hypergraph with chromatic number $3$ (that is, there is a $3$-colouring of the vertices of $G$ such that no edge is monochromatic).

Suppose any two edges of $G$ have a non-empty intersection. Must $G$ contain $O(r^2)$ many vertices? Must there be two edges which meet in $\gg r$ many vertices?

## Site Remarks
A problem of Erd\H{o}s and Shelah. The Fano geometry gives an example where there are no two edges which meet in $r-1$ vertices. Are there any other examples?

Erd\H{o}s and Lov\'{a}sz \cite{ErLo75} proved that there must be two edges which meet in $\gg \frac{r}{\log r}$ many vertices.

Alon has provided the following counterexample to the first question: as vertices take two sets $X$ and $Y$ of sizes $2r-2$ and $\frac{1}{2}\binom{2r-2}{r-1}$ respectively, where $Y$ corresponds to all partitions of $X$ into two equal parts. The edges are all subsets of $X$ of size $r$, and also all sets consisting of a subset of $X$ of size $r-1$ together with the unique element of $Y$ corresponding to the induced partition of $X$.

This hypergraph is intersecting, its chromatic number is $3$, and it has $\asymp 4^r/\sqrt{r}$ many vertices.

## Local Computation
- Implemented finite construction checks for Alon-style family at $r=3,4,5,6$.
- Verified pairwise-intersection property directly.
- For small instances ($r=3,4$), brute-force coloring search confirms not 2-colorable and 3-colorable.

- Construction sizes grow quickly: e.g. $(|V|,|E|)=(16,35)$ at $r=4$, $(43,126)$ at $r=5$.
- Finite checks match the role of this family as a counterexample to the $O(r^2)$ vertex bound.
- The second question (forcing $\gg r$ intersections) remains unresolved by these computations.

## Local Proof Attempts
The current evidence is purely density-based: count how much of \([1,N]\) is covered by representations \(2^k+n\) with \(\Omega(n)\le r\). The rapid empirical climb toward density 1 for small \(r\) is striking, but it remains only finite evidence for the near-full-density conjecture.
