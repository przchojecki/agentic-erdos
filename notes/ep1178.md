# EP-1178

## Problem Statement
For $r\geq 3$ let $d_r(e)$ be the minimal $d$ such that\[\mathrm{ex}_r(n,\mathcal{F})=o(n^2),\]where $\mathcal{F}$ is the family of $r$-uniform hypergraphs on $d$ vertices with $e$ edges.

Prove that\[d_r(e)=(r-2)e+3\]for all $r,e\geq 3$.

## Site Remarks
A conjecture of Brown, Erd\H{o}s, and S\'{o}s \cite{BES73}, who proved that $d_r(e)\geq (r-2)e+3$ (see also [1076]).

Ruzsa and Szemer\'{e}di \cite{RuSz78} proved $d_3(3)=6$ (see [716]).

Erd\H{o}s, Frankl, and R\"{o}dl \cite{EFR86} proved\[d_r(3)= (r-2)3+3\]for all $r\geq 3$. S\'{a}rk\"{o}zy and Selkow \cite{SaSe05} proved\[d_r(e) \leq (r-2)e+2+\lfloor \log_2 e\rfloor\]for all $r,e\geq 3$. Solymosi and Solymosi \cite{SoSo17} proved that $d_3(10)\leq 14$. Conlon, Gishboliner, Levanzov, and Shapira \cite{CGLS23} proved\[d_3(e)\leq e+O\left(\frac{\log e}{\log\log e}\right)\]for all $e\geq 3$.

In \cite{Er75b} Erd\H{o}s even asks whether, if $\mathcal{F}$ is the family of $3$-uniform hypergraphs on $k$ vertices with $k-3$ edges,\[\mathrm{ex}_3(n,\mathcal{F})\asymp n r_{k-3}(n),\]where $r_{k-3}(n)$ is the maximal size of a subset of $\{1,\ldots,n\}$ that does not contain a non-trivial arithmetic progression of length $k-3$. He states that Ruzsa has proved the lower bound for $k=6,7,8$.

See [1157] for the general case.
