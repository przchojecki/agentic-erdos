# EP-1061

## Problem Statement
How many solutions are there to\[\sigma(a)+\sigma(b)=\sigma(a+b)\]with $a+b\leq x$, where $\sigma$ is the sum of divisors function? Is it $\sim cx$ for some constant $c>0$?

## Local Proof Attempts
The exact counts show clear growth, but there is not yet enough structure to identify an asymptotic main term or constant. The problem remains to convert these finite profiles into a theorem about the order of growth of the number of pairs with \(a+b\le x\).
