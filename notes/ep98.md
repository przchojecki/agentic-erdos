# EP-98

## Problem Statement
Let $h(n)$ be such that any $n$ points in $\mathbb{R}^2$, with no three on a line and no four on a circle, determine at least $h(n)$ distinct distances. Does $h(n)/n\to \infty$?

## Site Remarks
Erd\H{o}s could not even prove $h(n)\geq n$. Pach has shown $h(n)<n^{\log_23}$. Erd\H{o}s, F\"{u}redi, and Pach \cite{EFPR93} have improved this to\[h(n) < n\exp(c\sqrt{\log n})\]for some constant $c>0$.

## Local Proof Attempts
Route:
Tried to leverage the general-position assumptions (no three collinear, no four cocircular) to force superlinear growth in the number of distinct distances.

Hard point:
Current incidence/energy methods do not bridge the large gap between near-linear upper constructions and the desired superlinear lower bound.

Target:
Under general position constraints (no three collinear, no four cocircular),
prove
\[
h(n)/n\to\infty
\]
for distinct distances.
Step A: Restricted incidence energy
Exploit forbidden collinearity/cocircularity to sharpen distance-energy upper
bounds beyond unrestricted sets.
Step B: Near-extremal structure theorem
Show any set with \(h(n)=O(n)\) must contain many forbidden local patterns.
Step C: Contradiction
Combine Step A and B to rule out linear distinct-distance growth.
Concrete Blocking Lemma (Most Critical):
Need a quantitative classification of low-distance-energy configurations under
the double general-position constraint.

Proposition W1 (Energy lower bound via Cauchy):
If \(D(P)\) is number of distinct distances for \(|P|=n\), and \(m_t\) are
distance multiplicities, then
\[
\sum_t m_t^2\ge \frac{\binom{n}{2}^2}{D(P)}.
\]
\[
\textit{Proof.}
\]
Cauchy-Schwarz on \(\sum_t m_t\).
\(\square\)
Proposition W2 (Reduction criterion):
If one can prove for general-position \(P\):
\[
\sum_t m_t^2\le \frac{n^3}{\omega(n)}
\]
for some \(\omega(n)\to\infty\), then
\[
D(P)\ge \omega(n)\,n,
\]
hence \(h(n)/n\to\infty\).
\[
\textit{Proof.}
\]
Combine W1 with \(\binom{n}{2}\asymp n^2\).
\(\square\)
