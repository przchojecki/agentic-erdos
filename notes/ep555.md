# EP-555

## Problem Statement
Let $R_k(G)$ denote the minimal $m$ such that if the edges of $K_m$ are $k$-coloured then there is a monochromatic copy of $G$. Determine the value of\[R_k(C_{2n}).\]

## Site Remarks
A problem of Erd\H{o}s and Graham. Erd\H{o}s \cite{Er81c} gives the bounds\[k^{1+\frac{1}{2n}}\ll R_k(C_{2n})\ll k^{1+\frac{1}{n-1}}.\]Chung and Graham \cite{ChGr75} showed that\[R_k(C_4)>k^2-k+1\]when $k-1$ is a prime power and\[R_k(C_4)\leq k^2+k+1\]for all $k$.

This problem is #24 in Ramsey Theory (https://mathweb.ucsd.edu/~erdosproblems/erdos/newproblems/MulticolorREvenCycles.html) in the graphs problem collection.
