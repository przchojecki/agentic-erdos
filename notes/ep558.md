# EP-558

## Problem Statement
Let $R_k(G)$ denote the minimal $m$ such that if the edges of $K_m$ are $k$-coloured then there is a monochromatic copy of $G$. Determine\[R_k(K_{s,t})\]where $K_{s,t}$ is the complete bipartite graph with $s$ vertices in one component and $t$ in the other.

## Site Remarks
Chung and Graham \cite{ChGr75} prove the general bounds\[(2\pi\sqrt{st})^{\frac{1}{s+t}}\left(\frac{s+t}{e^2}\right)k^{\frac{st-1}{s+t}}\leq R_k(K_{s,t})\leq (t-1)(k+k^{1/s})^s\]and determined\[R_k(K_{2,2})=(1+o(1))k^2.\]Alon, R\'{o}nyai, and Szab\'{o} \cite{ARS99} have proved that\[R_k(K_{3,3})=(1+o(1))k^3\]and that if $s\geq (t-1)!+1$ then\[R_k(K_{s,t})\asymp k^t.\]This problem is #27 in Ramsey Theory (https://mathweb.ucsd.edu/~erdosproblems/erdos/newproblems/MulticolorRKst.html) in the graphs problem collection.

## Local Computation
Finite k-color K_n simulations were run with explicit detection of monochromatic K_{s,t}. The observed thresholds are informative in tiny instances but do not give a complete formula.

## Local Proof Attempts
Several major subfamilies are asymptotically determined, but no complete uniform formula is known for all fixed \(s,t\).

The problem remains open in general, and the finite deep profile only shows threshold-like behavior in small instances.
