# EP-208

## Problem Statement
Let $s_1<s_2<\cdots$ be the sequence of squarefree numbers. Is it true that, for any $\epsilon>0$ and large $n$,\[s_{n+1}-s_n \ll_\epsilon s_n^{\epsilon}?\]Is it true that\[s_{n+1}-s_n \leq (1+o(1))\frac{\pi^2}{6}\frac{\log s_n}{\log\log s_n}?\]

## Site Remarks
Erd\H{o}s \cite{Er51} showed that there are infinitely many $n$ such that\[s_{n+1}-s_n > (1+o(1))\frac{\pi^2}{6}\frac{\log s_n}{\log\log s_n},\]so this bound would be the best possible.

In \cite{Er79} Erd\H{o}s says perhaps $s_{n+1}-s_n \ll \log s_n$, but he is 'very doubtful'.

Filaseta and Trifonov \cite{FiTr92} proved an upper bound of $s_n^{1/5+o(1)}$. Pandey \cite{Pa24} has improved this exponent to $1/5-c$ for some constant $c>0$.

Granville \cite{Gr98} showed that $s_{n+1}-s_n\ll_\epsilon s_n^\epsilon$ for all $\epsilon>0$ follows from the ABC conjecture (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Abc_conjecture).

See also [489] and [145]. A more general form of this problem is given in [1101].
