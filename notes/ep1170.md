# EP-1170

## Problem Statement
Is it consistent that\[\omega_2\to (\alpha)_2^2\]for every $\alpha <\omega_2$?

## Site Remarks
Laver \cite{La82} proved the consistency of $\omega_2\to (\omega_12+1,\alpha)^2$ for all $\alpha<\omega_2$. Foreman and Hajnal \cite{FoHa03} proved the consistency of $\omega_2\to (\omega_1^2+1,\alpha)^2$ for all $\alpha<\omega_2$.
